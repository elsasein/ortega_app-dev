**MOVIE SERIES TITLE: Mission Impossible**
> An American series of action spy films, based on the 1966 television series created by Bruce Geller. The series is mainly produced by Tom Cruise, who plays Ethan Hunt, an agent of the Impossible Missions Force. It's an action spy-thriller series following Ethan Hunt (*played by Tom Cruise*), a senior field operative for the Impossible Missions Force (IMF), a clandestine U.S. government agency that handles high-stakes, off-the-books operations
---
**🎬 Film Series**
1. Mission: Impossible (1996) - Brian De Palma
  >An American agent, under false suspicion of disloyalty, must discover and expose the real spy without the help of his organization.
2. Mission: Impossible 2 (2000) - John Woo
  >MF agent Ethan Hunt is sent to Sydney to find and destroy a genetically modified disease called "Chimera".
3. Mission: Impossible III (2006) - J.J. Abrams
  > IMF agent Ethan Hunt comes into conflict with a dangerous and sadistic arms dealer who threatens his life and his fiancée in response.
4. Mission: Impossible Ghost Protocol (2011) - Brad Bird
  >The IMF is shut down when it's implicated in the bombing of the Kremlin, causing Ethan Hunt and his new team to go rogue to clear their organization's name.
5. Mission: Impossible Rogue Nation (2015) - Christopher McQuarrie
  >Ethan and his team take on their most impossible mission yet when they have to eradicate an international rogue organization as highly skilled as they are and committed to destroying the IMF.
6. Mission: Impossible Fallout (2018) - Christopher McQuarrie
  >A group of terrorists plans to detonate three plutonium cores for a simultaneous nuclear attack on different cities. Ethan Hunt, along with his IMF team, sets out to stop the carnage.
7. Mission: Impossible Dead Reckoning Part One (2023) - Christopher McQuarrie
  >Ethan Hunt and his IMF team must track down a terrifying new weapon that threatens all of humanity.
8. Mission: Impossible The Final Reckoning (2025) - Christopher McQuarrie
  >Hunt and the IMF pursue a dangerous AI called the Entity that's infiltrated global intelligence. With governments and a figure from his past in pursuit, Hunt races to stop it from forever changing the world.

**🎯THE CORE IMF TEAM**
| Character | Role |
| :----------- | :----------- |
| Ethan Hunt (*Tom Cruise*) | The maverick team leader and supreme field agent known for pulling off superhuman, practical stunt-heavy operations to protect the world. |
| Luther Stickell (*Ving Rhames*) |  Ethan’s most trusted associate and closest friend. He is a brilliant master hacker who has appeared in every single film alongside Cruise. |
| Benji Dunn (*Simon Pegg*) | Originally a quirky IMF lab technician, he transforms into a full field agent. He provides vital technical wizardry and much of the team’s comic relief. |

**🤝Crucial Allies & Former Team Members** 
|Character | Role |
| :----------- | :----------- |
| Ilsa Faust (*Rebecca Ferguson*) |  A deadly, disavowed former MI6 agent whose allegiances constantly shift. She acts as a powerful foil and romantic interest to Ethan across multiple films. |
| William Brandt (*Jeremy Renner*) | A highly capable IMF analyst and former field agent with a guarded past who protects the team in Ghost Protocol and Rogue Nation. |
| Alan Hunley (*Alec Baldwin*) |  Initially the CIA Director who forcefully tries to dismantle the IMF, he eventually becomes the IMF Secretary before a tragic sacrifice in Fallout. |
| Paris (*Pom Klementieff*) |  Originally a fierce, silent assassin working for the enemy, she switches sides to become a crucial physical ally to the team. |
